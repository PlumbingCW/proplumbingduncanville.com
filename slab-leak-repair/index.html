Emergency Slab Leak Repair in Duncanville, TX

A leak under the foundation stays hidden until a bill, a warm floor or a damp spot gives it away, and we find it without a demolition.

Call (972) 903-8224

A slab leak is a leak in a water pipe that runs under or inside a concrete foundation. It is hard to see and expensive when ignored, because the water can undermine soil, lift flooring and run a meter for weeks. If you can see water on the floor or the meter is spinning fast, shut off the main valve and call the emergency line, which is open 24/7.

Slab leak repair means finding the exact spot, then choosing among a spot repair, a reroute around the leak or a replacement of the line. The repair is chosen to cause the least damage and to last. We explain each option and give a clear price before work starts.

Emergency signs of a slab leak

  • A warm patch on the floor, often from a hot water line
  • The sound of running water when everything is off
  • A water meter that moves with all fixtures closed
  • A sudden jump in the water bill
  • Wet carpet or lifting laminate with no source
  • Cracks in tile or walls that appear along a line
  • Low hot water pressure with normal cold pressure

A leak under the slab will not stop on its own. Hot water leaks tend to show first because they warm the concrete. If the floor is hot under your feet, shut off the water heater and the main and call.

Why slab leaks happen in the Dallas area

Much of the Dallas region sits on expansive clay. According to the USDA Official Series Description, Houston Black clay, a Blackland Prairie clay soil, forms cracks 0.5 to 4 inches wide in dry periods, to a depth of a foot or more, and that cracks stay open for 90 to 150 days in most years.

We cite it as an example of clay soils of this type, common in the Dallas region, not as a map of any single lot.

When clay shrinks and swells, the slab can shift by small amounts, and pipes cast in or under it can rub, kink or break at a joint. Copper in contact with concrete can also corrode. Homes built in the growth decades may have supply lines in or under the slab, and a plumber can tell you what yours has.

What decides the price of a slab leak repair

Slab leaks vary widely. You get a clear price before work starts.

  • Depth and locationA leak under a kitchen cabinet or a tiled area takes more work to reach.
  • MethodA reroute through the attic can cost more or less than a spot repair, depending on the house.
  • Floor restorationConcrete patch and floor covering are separate costs.
  • Number of leaksMore than one leak often points to a repipe.

Ask for a written scope and ask who handles the floor repair afterwards.

Insurance and documentation

Some homeowner policies cover the repair of the pipe or the damage that follows, and some do not. Ask your insurer early, keep the leak detection report and the invoice, and take photographs before the floor is opened. A written report from the plumber describes the cause and the repair, which an adjuster often asks for.
