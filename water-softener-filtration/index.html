Water Softener and Filtration in Duncanville, TX

Scale, taste and spots are fixed by treatment that matches your water, and the first step is always a tap test.

Call (972) 903-8224

Water softeners and filters do different jobs. A softener swaps calcium and magnesium for sodium or potassium, which reduces scale on fixtures and in water heaters. A filter removes particles, chlorine taste and odor. Many homes need one of them, some need both and some need neither. The honest first step is to test the water coming from your tap.

We install whole house softeners, carbon filters, sediment filters, under sink units and reverse osmosis systems for drinking water. We also advise when a system is not needed, because buying equipment for a problem you do not have is a waste.

What Duncanville water looks like

Duncanville buys treated surface water from the City of Dallas. According to Dallas Water Utilities, the supply draws from seven sources, including the Elm Fork of the Trinity River and Lakes Grapevine, Lewisville, Ray Hubbard, Ray Roberts, Tawakoni and Fork.

The disinfectant is chloramine, which averaged 2.16 parts per million in the 2022 report, with a range from 0.60 to 3.7. Lead and copper testing found none of 30 sites over the action level.

The report we reviewed does not give a hardness figure, so we do not claim one. To learn your hardness, test your tap with a kit or a lab, and ask Dallas Water Utilities for its most recent annual water quality report. Public Works at (972) 780-4900 can answer questions about water quality in the city.

Signs your water may need treatment

  • White crusting on faucets and showerheads
  • Spots on glasses after the dishwasher
  • A short life for water heaters and kettles
  • A chlorine or chloramine taste you dislike
  • Dry skin or soap that does not lather
  • Brown stains near fixtures from iron or old pipe

These signs have several possible causes. Scale suggests hardness. A chlorine taste points to a carbon filter. Brown stains can come from your own pipes rather than the supply. A test tells which.

What decides the price of water treatment

Treatment systems range widely. You get a clear price before work starts.

  • Type of systemSoftener, whole house filter and RO each carry a different cost.
  • CapacityA larger household or higher hardness needs a larger unit.
  • InstallationLocation, drain access and wiring affect labor.
  • Ongoing costSalt, filters and membranes cost money over time, and we explain it.

Ask about total ownership cost, not only the purchase price. Beware of any seller who quotes a system without testing your water.

Softener caveats

Softened water has more sodium or potassium. People on a low sodium diet should ask a doctor, and drinking water can be taken from an unsoftened tap or an RO unit. Softeners also discharge brine to the drain, and some communities restrict that, so we confirm local rules. Softened water may feel slick, and that is normal.

Water treatment and Duncanville homes

Because the disinfectant used by Dallas is chloramine, a carbon filter should be selected for chloramine reduction, and its label should say so. Many ordinary filters are rated for chlorine only. Chloramine also affects aquarium owners and kidney dialysis patients, who need special treatment.
