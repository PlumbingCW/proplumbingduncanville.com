Privacy Policy

How we handle information from this website and our phone line.

Call (972) 903-8224

This policy explains what information Duncanville Pro Plumbing may collect when you visit this website or call our line, and how it is used. Last updated November 16, 2026.

Information we collect

This website has no forms and does not ask you to create an account. Our servers and hosting provider may log standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit. When you call us, we collect the phone number you call from and the details you give about your plumbing problem and address.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. By continuing a call, you agree to the recording.

How we use information

  • To answer your call, dispatch a plumber and complete the job
  • To give you a price, confirm an appointment and follow up on the work
  • To improve our service and train our staff
  • To keep the website secure and working

Sharing

We share information with the plumbers and dispatchers who need it to serve you and with service providers that help us run the website and phone line. We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information when the law requires it.

Cookies and analytics

The website may use basic cookies or analytics tools to count visits and see which pages are useful. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

Retention and security

We keep call records and job information for as long as we need them for service, legal and accounting purposes. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no system is perfectly secure.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it where the law allows. Write to contact@proplumbingduncanville.com or call (972) 903-8224.
