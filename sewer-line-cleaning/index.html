Emergency Sewer Line Cleaning in Duncanville, TX

A blocked main line sends waste to the lowest drain in the house, and we clear it with heavy cable and jetting equipment.

Call (972) 903-8224

A blocked main sewer line sends waste back into the lowest fixture in the house, usually a floor drain, a shower or a ground level toilet. Our 24/7 line is staffed for exactly this call. Stop flushing, hold off on laundry, and tell dispatch how many fixtures are affected, because that decides which machine goes on the truck.

Sewer line cleaning is the work of clearing the main drain that runs from the house to the public connection. It uses a large cutting machine or a jetter, and it ends with a flow test. A camera pass is added when the cause is not obvious.

Main line blockage: when it is an emergency

A single slow tub is a fixture problem. A toilet that gurgles when the washing machine drains is a main line problem, because both fixtures share the same pipe downstream. If you lift a toilet cap and see water rising when another fixture runs, the main is restricted.

The main line is bigger than a branch, commonly 4 inches, and a clog there needs a machine with a heavier cable and a bigger cutting head. A small auger bought at a hardware store will not reach it.

Common causes we find

  • Tree roots entering through joints in clay or cast iron
  • Grease and soap scale built up over years
  • Wipes sold as flushable that do not break down
  • A bellied section of pipe that holds water and solids
  • Debris from a prior break or a failed repair

In Duncanville homes from the growth decades, cast iron laterals were common. Roots find hairline cracks in those joints and grow into hair like masses that catch everything. Cleaning clears them, and a camera tells you whether the pipe will need a repair soon.

How a main line is cleaned

  1. 01Find the cleanoutWe open the cleanout outside or pull a toilet if none exists, and test the flow before starting.
  2. 02Cut and clearA cutting head on a heavy cable clears roots and compacted waste. Jetting follows when grease is the cause.
  3. 03Flush and verifyWe send water down the line and watch it drain from the cleanout and from the house fixtures.
  4. 04Camera lookWhen the blockage returns or the cause is unclear, a camera shows the pipe condition and the next step.

Cleaning clears the pipe. It does not repair it. If the camera shows a crack, an offset joint or a collapsed section, the pipe needs sewer line repair, and repeated clogs from grease respond best to hydro jetting. We tell you which of those fits before the machine is shut off.

What decides the price of sewer line cleaning

Pricing depends on the line and the machine. You get a clear price before work starts.

  • Line lengthThe distance from the cleanout to the blockage sets cable length and time.
  • Type of blockageA soft clog opens quickly. A thick root mass takes more cutting and more passes.
  • Tools neededA cutter, a jetter or both. A camera pass is separate.
  • Hour of the visitNights, weekends and holidays carry an after-hours rate that is quoted first.

Texas does not regulate plumbing prices, so ask for the figure in writing before agreeing to any work.

Preventing the next backup

Keep wipes, paper towels and grease out of the drain. Put a strainer in every shower and tub. If a camera shows roots, treat it as a clock rather than a one time event, because roots return unless the entry point is repaired. Many homeowners schedule a yearly cleaning on a line that has a history, and it costs far less than a weekend backup.
