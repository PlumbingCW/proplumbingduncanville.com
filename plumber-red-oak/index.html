A fast growing Ellis County city and its plumbing

Red Oak sits in Ellis County, south of Duncanville along I-35E, and it has grown fast. According to the Handbook of Texas, the census counts show 1,882 people in 1980, 4,301 in 2000 and 14,222 in 2020.

That is more than a threefold jump in twenty years, and most of the new housing is recent. If you own one of those homes, your pipes are modern, and the usual calls are about water heaters, fittings and builder grade fixtures.

If a pipe is spraying or a tank is leaking, close the main valve and call our 24/7 line. We dispatch to Red Oak every day, including nights and holidays, and we tell you at the start of the call whether we are in range from Duncanville.

Old Red Oak and the new subdivisions

Red Oak was incorporated in 1949, but the community is much older. It was founded around 1844 on Red Oak Creek, settled by the Patton family, and the railroad arrived in 1884. The older core has houses with old plumbing, and the newer subdivisions around it use plastic pipes.

Both sides have typical problems. In older houses, cast iron and galvanized lines fail from age. In newer houses, we see water heaters that were sized tightly for the home, pressure regulators that were never set and outdoor faucets that were never insulated. A visit starts by learning which kind of house we are in.
