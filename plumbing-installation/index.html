Plumbing Installation in Duncanville, TX

New fixtures, remodel rough in and appliance hookups are planned, permitted and tested before the walls close.

Call (972) 903-8224

Plumbing installation covers new work: adding a bathroom, moving a sink, setting a new tub, connecting a dishwasher or an icemaker, running a gas line for a range or putting in an outdoor hose bib. It differs from repair because it begins with a plan. The pipe sizes, vent paths and drains must be right before the first wall is closed.

The best time to call is before demolition. A plumber can look at the existing lines and tell you what a layout will cost, which can change how a remodel is designed. Moving a toilet by a few feet may cost little. Moving it across the house can be a major job.

What we install

  • Sinks, faucets and kitchen or bath fixtures
  • Toilets, tubs and showers, including rough in work
  • Dishwasher, icemaker and washing machine connections
  • Garbage disposals and pot fillers
  • Outdoor hose bibs and sprinkler hookups
  • Water softener or filter connections
  • Water heater and gas appliance hookups

Each has its own code requirements. A shower drain needs the right trap and slope. A dishwasher drain needs an air gap or a high loop. A gas appliance needs a shutoff and a leak tested connection.

Remodel plumbing, step by step

  1. 01Plan the layoutWe review drawings or measure the room and decide where supply, drain and vent lines will run.
  2. 02Rough inPipes are installed in the open wall or floor, pressure tested and left for inspection.

Permits and inspections

Most new plumbing needs a permit and an inspection. The City of Duncanville adopted the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC), and permit rules vary by project, so we confirm the requirement with the city. Contractors register with the city through the Citizen Access Portal.

Do your own check before you hire. Ask for the Responsible Master Plumber license number and look it up at tsbpe.texas.gov. The license number begins with the letter M, and the board shows whether it is active.

What decides the price of a new installation

Installation is scoped to the job. You get a clear written price before work starts.

  • ScopeA single fixture is a quick job. A new bathroom involves rough in, fixtures and tie ins.
  • AccessOpen framing is easy. A slab requires cutting concrete for a new drain.
  • MaterialsFixture selection, pipe type and valves all change the total.
  • Permit and inspectionRequired in most new work, and quoted with the job.

Compare written scopes. A low price that omits the permit or fixtures is not a low price.

Slabs, drains and older homes

Many North Texas homes are slab on grade, so a new drain in a ground floor addition often means cutting the concrete and then patching it. We plan the cut to avoid existing lines and mark it with a locator before breaking the slab.

In older homes, existing drains may be cast iron or clay. We check the connection point and, when it makes sense, run a camera through the line before tying a new fixture into it. An addition that overloads an old drain is a problem waiting to happen.
