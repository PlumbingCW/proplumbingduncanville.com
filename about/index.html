About Duncanville Pro Plumbing

A plain account of how we work and how to check any plumber.

Call (972) 903-8224

Duncanville Pro Plumbing is a plumbing service based at 511 E Camp Wisdom Rd in Duncanville, Texas. We answer a 24/7 emergency line and schedule planned work for homes and businesses in Duncanville, DeSoto, Cedar Hill, Lancaster, Grand Prairie and Red Oak.

We keep this page factual. We do not publish invented years in business, customer counts, awards or reviews, because none of them would help you choose a plumber. What we can tell you is how we work and how to check anyone you hire.

How we work

  • The 24/7 line is answered every day of the year, and a licensed plumber is dispatched as soon as one is free.
  • You get a clear price before work starts, including on after hours calls.
  • Same day appointments are offered when the schedule allows.
  • Plumbers are licensed and insured, and you can ask for the Responsible Master Plumber license number.
  • We say when a repair is not worth it, and when a cheaper fix will do.

How to verify any plumber in Texas

The Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners licenses plumbers. Ask the company for the Responsible Master Plumber license number, which begins with the letter M, and look it up at tsbpe.texas.gov. The board does not regulate prices, so ask for a written estimate. The same site has a complaint page if something goes wrong. Do this with every plumber you consider, including us.

Why local knowledge matters

Duncanville was incorporated on August 2, 1947, and grew from 841 residents in 1950 to 35,008 in 1990. Many homes date from those decades, and so do their drains, supply lines and water heaters.

The soil is clay of a type common in the Dallas region, which shifts with wet and dry seasons and stresses buried pipe. Water comes from the City of Dallas and carries chloramine. These facts shape the way we diagnose a call.

We know the roads too. Camp Wisdom Road, Wheatland Road, Main Street, US 67 and I-20 are how our trucks move, and Armstrong Park, the Recreation Center and the Public Library are the places people give as landmarks when they call.

What to expect on a visit

When you call, dispatch asks what is happening, where you are and whether anyone is at risk. For active water, we talk you through closing the main valve before anything else. A licensed plumber is sent as soon as one is free, and you are told whether the visit is an emergency call or a scheduled one.

On site, the plumber looks at the problem before proposing a fix. You get the cause in plain words, the options with their trade-offs and a clear price before work starts. If a part is not on the truck, you hear that too. When the job is done, the plumber tests the repair, cleans up and explains what was done and what to watch for.

Emergency work and planned work

Emergency calls are the reason the line is open 24/7. A burst pipe, a sewer backup, a failed water heater or a hidden leak that is flooding a floor cannot wait for a business day. Planned work is different. Installations, repiping, camera inspections and water treatment are scheduled for a time that suits you, and same day appointments are offered when the calendar allows.

We do not turn a routine call into an emergency upsell, and we do not turn an emergency into a lecture. If the fix is small, we say so. If the problem is bigger than it looked, we show you why.
