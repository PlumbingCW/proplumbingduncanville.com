Emergency Leak Detection in Duncanville, TX

Hidden leaks do their damage quietly, so we locate them with meter tests, listening gear and thermal imaging before anyone cuts into a wall.

Call (972) 903-8224

Hidden leaks cause the worst damage because nobody sees them until the bill, the smell or the floor tells the story. If you can see water spreading, shut off the main valve at the house and call the emergency line, which is open 24/7. If the leak is slow, a plumber can usually come the same day.

Leak detection is the work of finding exactly where water is escaping before anyone cuts into a wall or breaks a slab. The tools are listening equipment, pressure testing, thermal imaging and meter checks. The aim is a small, accurate opening rather than a large, hopeful one.

Signs of a hidden leak

  • A water bill that rose with no change in habits
  • A water meter that moves when every fixture is off
  • Warm spots on the floor, or hot water that runs long to reach a tap
  • The sound of running water when nothing is in use
  • Cracks in drywall, damp baseboards or musty odor
  • Lush or soggy patches in the yard along the water line

Emergency: when a leak cannot wait

Water in an electrical area, a ceiling that is sagging, or flooring that is lifting needs attention now. Shut off the main valve, switch off power to wet rooms at the breaker only if you can reach the panel safely, and keep people away from wet outlets. Then call, and describe where the water is.

For a slow leak, the same day call is usually enough. Do not leave a suspected leak for weeks, because the meter keeps counting and the damage grows quietly.

How we pinpoint a leak

  1. 01Meter testWe close every fixture and watch the meter. If it moves, water is leaving the system.
  2. 02Isolate the sectionShutting valves one at a time tells us whether the leak is on the hot side, the cold side or the yard line.
  3. 03Listen and scanAcoustic gear picks up the sound of escaping water. Thermal imaging shows warm or cool patches.

Water meter leaks and high bills

A high bill is often the first clue. Look at your meter with every faucet off, including ice makers and sprinklers. Check the leak indicator on the dial, which spins when any water flows. If it moves, the loss is on your side of the meter. Toilet flappers that seep silently are the most common cause, and a plumber can check that in minutes.

If the dial is still after a toilet dye test, the leak is in a buried pipe or a slab line. That is the point where listening equipment earns its cost.

Water service line leaks

The pipe that runs from the meter to your house is the service line, and it is usually your responsibility.

The City of Duncanville completed a water service line survey on November 13, 2024, with extra attention on homes built before 1988, and the city describes its lines as primarily non lead or unknown. If you want to know what your service line is made of, call Public Works at (972) 780-4900.

A leak on the service line shows as soggy ground, a drop in pressure or a meter that runs with the house shut off. Repair is a trench, a bore or a replacement of the run, and we explain the option that fits your yard.

What decides the price of leak detection

Detection is billed separately from the repair, so you know where the leak is before you decide on a fix.

  • ComplexityA single stained wall is simpler than a whole house with no visible clue.
  • EquipmentAcoustic and thermal tools are used together on hard cases.
  • AccessOpen crawlspaces and attic hatches save time.
  • Repair afterOpening and patching is a second step with its own price, given before work.

You get a clear price before work starts and we will not open a wall until the location is confirmed.

Duncanville ground and pipe notes

Many North Texas homes sit on concrete slabs over clay soil that moves with wet and dry cycles. That movement can stress water lines where they pass through a slab or a wall, which is why under slab and yard lines are common leak locations in this region.

If your water bill spikes, you can also call Utility Billing at (972) 780-5010 to confirm the meter reading, and the city's after hours water emergency line is (972) 780-4959.

We perform leak work for Cedar Hill and DeSoto households too, and a confirmed pipe leak may lead to repiping if the line has failed in more than one place.

Leak detection covers Duncanville ZIP codes 75116, 75137 and 75138 and reaches Lancaster ZIP 75134 and 75146. According to the Dallas Water Utilities 2022 water quality report, the water reaching your meter is treated with chloramine, so a leak on your side of the meter shows up on your own bill.
