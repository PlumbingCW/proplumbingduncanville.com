Emergency Toilet Repair in Duncanville, TX

Overflowing, clogged or leaking toilets are fixed fast, and we install new ones when replacing is the smarter move.

Call (972) 903-8224

An overflowing toilet needs action in seconds. Lift the tank lid, push the flapper down to stop the fill, or turn the shutoff valve behind the toilet clockwise to close it. Then call our 24/7 emergency line. A toilet that keeps rising after a flush is either a clog in the trap or a blockage further down the line, and a plumber can tell which.

Toilet repair covers clogs, leaks at the base, wobbling bowls, flappers, fill valves, flush valves, cracked tanks and worn out seals. When a toilet is cracked or chronically weak, replacement is more sensible, and we do that too.

Toilet emergencies and what to do first

  1. Stop the water. Close the shutoff valve behind the toilet.
  2. Do not flush again. Each flush sends more water toward the blockage.
  3. Soak up spills with towels. Keep children away from contaminated water.
  4. Call the 24/7 line and tell dispatch if other fixtures are slow or bubbling.

If only the toilet overflows, the blockage is probably in the trap or a short branch. If a tub or sink gurgles at the same time, the blockage may be in the main line, and that is the situation where sewer line cleaning is the real fix.

Why toilets fail in older homes

Toilets are simple, but the parts are not forever. Rubber flappers soften, fill valves clog with debris and wax rings flatten. Homes built in the growth decades have often had a toilet or two swapped by a previous owner, and a mismatched flange height is a common source of a leak that returns after a repair.

If you have a toilet from the 1980s or earlier, it may use far more water per flush than a modern unit. Replacing it can reduce water use and solve a persistent clog problem at the same time.

What decides the price of toilet repair

Most toilet jobs are quick, and a clear price comes before work starts.

  • Repair or replaceA flapper or fill valve is a small job. Pulling and resetting a toilet or replacing it costs more.
  • The clogA trap clog clears quickly. A deeper blockage becomes a drain job.
  • Parts and modelOlder or specialty toilets may need unusual parts.
  • Time of dayAfter hours calls carry a quoted rate.

Ask for the price in writing. Texas does not regulate plumbing prices.

Installing a new toilet

A new toilet takes careful work: a flat flange, a new wax ring or a no wax seal, a level set and a supply line that fits. We remove and haul the old fixture, check the floor for soft spots and test for leaks and flush before leaving.

Height matters. Comfort height bowls, elongated bowls and compact units all fit different spaces. Tell us about the room and who uses it, and we help choose.

Water use and toilets in Duncanville

Duncanville buys its water from the City of Dallas, and the city bills through Utility Billing at (972) 780-5010. A running toilet can waste a large amount of water, which is why it shows up on the bill before it shows up anywhere else.

If your toilet is in an older bathroom and you notice a musty smell or a soft floor near it, tell us so we can check for a long term leak at the flange.

A toilet issue sometimes belongs to a larger problem. See drain cleaning for blocked branches and faucet repair for fixtures in the same bathroom. We also respond in Lancaster and Cedar Hill.

Toilet repairs are dispatched across ZIP codes 75116, 75137 and 75138 and to DeSoto ZIP 75115 and Lancaster ZIP 75146. Replacement toilets are set under the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC) that Duncanville adopted, and permit rules vary by job.
