Our base is at 511 E Camp Wisdom Rd in Duncanville, and our 24/7 emergency line serves the home city plus five nearby towns. Dispatch confirms range by street address, since a call at the edge of the area may take longer.

Duncanville

We serve every address in Duncanville, ZIP codes 75116, 75137 and 75138. According to the Handbook of Texas, Duncanville grew from 841 people in 1950 to 40,706 in 2020.

The city sits in Dallas County with I-20 across the north and US 67 through the east. Landmarks such as Armstrong Park, the Recreation Center and Main Street are in our regular route, and the Forest Hills neighborhood is one of the areas where we work.

Landmarks we use when you call

Dispatch often asks for a landmark, because it is faster than spelling a street. In Duncanville the usual ones are Armstrong Park beside City Hall on E. Wheatland Road, the Recreation Center and the Public Library on Main Street, and the Camp Wisdom Road corridor, where our own address sits at 511 E Camp Wisdom Rd.

The city counts 17 parks and almost 5 miles of trails, and the Forest Hills neighborhood is one of the named areas where we work.

Giving a cross street and a landmark helps the plumber find a house that sits on a short or curving street. It also helps when a phone location is unclear. If you do not know the nearest cross street, tell dispatch which park, school or shopping center is closest.

How we decide what is in range

Range is about roads, not straight lines. A town that looks close on a map can sit behind a river, a county line or a stretch of congested highway, and dispatch thinks about the route a truck would actually drive. We confirm every address at the start of the call and tell you plainly if a visit would be slower than usual.

Emergency calls take priority for dispatch. If water is spreading or a line is backing up, the call is handled first, and we talk you through the main valve while the plumber is on the way. Planned work such as an inspection, a repipe or a water heater installation is scheduled at a time that suits you, and range matters less.

What a call from the edge of the range looks like

A caller in Red Oak or north Grand Prairie hears the same opening questions as a caller on Main Street in Duncanville: what is happening, where the water is going and who is at risk. The difference is the route.

Dispatch checks which plumber is closest, whether the roads are clear and whether the job is urgent enough to go first, then calls you back with a plain answer. If the visit will take longer than usual, you hear that before you agree to anything.

Why each town has its own page

Towns have different housing, different water and different soil. DeSoto and Lancaster have large stocks of older homes, Cedar Hill has hills and a lake shore, Grand Prairie reports hard water and reaches into three counties and Red Oak is a fast growing Ellis County city.

A page that treats them all alike would not help anyone, so each town page explains what we see there, which ZIP codes it uses and what to do before we arrive.
