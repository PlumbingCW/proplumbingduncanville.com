Emergency Burst Pipe Repair in Duncanville, TX

Water pouring from a burst pipe needs fast action, so shut the main valve and call our 24/7 line.

A burst pipe can release hundreds of gallons in an hour, so speed matters. Close the main water valve at the house first. If you cannot find it, call the City of Duncanville after hours water emergency line at (972) 780-4959. Then call our 24/7 line, and a plumber is sent as soon as one is free.

After the water is off, open a faucet low in the house to drain the lines, switch off the water heater breaker or gas control, and move valuables away from the wet area. If water is near outlets or the breaker panel, stay clear and call. Photographs of the damage help your insurance claim.

What to do first when a pipe bursts

  1. Shut off the main water valve.
  2. Turn off the water heater, since it must not run dry.
  3. Open the lowest faucet and flush toilets to drain the lines.
  4. Keep away from wet outlets and the electrical panel.
  5. Call the 24/7 line and describe where the water is.
  6. Take photographs before any cleanup begins.

Do not try to thaw a frozen pipe with a torch. Use a hair dryer or warm towels on a pipe that is still intact, with the faucet open, and call a plumber if you cannot see the freeze point.

Why pipes burst in this region

Freeze events are less frequent in North Texas than in colder states, which means homes are often not prepared for them.

According to the City of Duncanville storm recovery page, Winter Storm Uri ran from February 13 to 17, 2021, and many North Texas pipes in attics, garages and exterior walls were hit by freezing temperatures. A pipe that freezes can split when the ice expands, and the leak only shows when it thaws.

Not every burst is caused by freezing. Corroded pipe, high pressure from a failed regulator, ground movement in clay soil and fittings that finally fail are common causes. In older copper and galvanized lines, a burst may be the first sign of a larger problem.

What decides the price of a burst pipe repair

The price depends on the failure. You get a clear price before work starts, even at night.

  • Pipe type and sizeCopper, PEX, CPVC and galvanized pipe each need different materials and tools.
  • AccessA pipe in an open attic is a short job. A pipe in a finished wall needs patching.
  • ExtentA single split is one repair. Several splits in a freeze can mean replacing a long run.
  • After hoursNight and holiday calls carry a rate that we quote first.
