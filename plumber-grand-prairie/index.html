Winter, summer and the plumbing calendar in Grand Prairie

North Texas weather asks plumbing to work in both directions. A hard freeze can split pipes in attics and garages, and long hot stretches push irrigation and outdoor faucet lines hard. Winter Storm Uri ran from February 13 to 17, 2021, and the memory of that week still shapes how many Grand Prairie homeowners think about insulation, shutoff valves and backup plans.

If a pipe bursts, close the main valve and call our 24/7 line. We dispatch to Grand Prairie every day, including nights and holidays, with priority on active water. Our base is in Duncanville, so we serve the south part of the city most easily, and we say plainly when a call is near the edge of our range.

Grand Prairie water is different, and hard

Grand Prairie buys surface water from Dallas, Fort Worth, Midlothian and Mansfield, and can add water from up to three Trinity Aquifer wells. According to the city's own water quality report, its water is hard, at 170 parts per million or 9.9 grains per gallon. Chloramine disinfectant averaged 2.95 parts per million in the report we reviewed.

Hard water leaves scale in water heaters, on fixtures and inside pipes. A tank heater in a hard water home benefits from a yearly flush, and a tankless heater needs a descaling schedule. If you are weighing a softener, our page on water softeners and filtration explains how to test first.
