Water Heater Installation in Duncanville, TX

Planning ahead gives you time to choose the right fuel, type and size, and we handle the connections, venting and testing.

Call (972) 903-8224

Water heater installation is the planned side of hot water: a new home, a remodel, a move to a different fuel or an upgrade before the old unit fails. Because nothing is leaking, you have time to choose the right type and size, and we can walk through each option on the phone.

An installation covers the unit, the supply and fuel connections, venting for gas, a relief valve with a discharge pipe, an expansion tank where needed, and a final test. We also take care of the pan and the drain when the heater sits above a finished floor.

Planning the install

  1. Pick the fuel. Gas is common where a line and vent exist. Electric is simpler when there is no gas.
  2. Pick the type. A tank is the lowest upfront cost. A hybrid heat pump unit saves energy. A tankless unit saves space.
  3. Size by households. We count bathrooms, people and the peak demand.
  4. Check the location. Clearance, venting, drains and electrical capacity all decide what fits.

New construction and remodels often change the location of the heater. That is when a good plan matters, because moving a gas line or a vent changes the cost and the permit needs.

Installation steps

  1. 01Walk the siteWe check the location, existing lines, venting and clearances before anything is ordered.
  2. 02Prepare and setThe pan, the stand if needed and the new unit are placed level and secure.
  3. 03Connect and ventWater, gas or electric and the vent are connected to the manufacturer's instructions and local rules.
  4. 04Test and handoverWe fill, purge air, check for leaks and combustion, and show you the shutoffs and the temperature setting.

What decides the price of an installation

Installs are priced to the home. You get a clear price before work starts.

  • Type and capacityTank, hybrid and tankless units are priced very differently.
  • Existing connectionsReusing a good vent and lines costs less than rerouting.
  • Code itemsExpansion tank, pan, discharge pipe, seismic or flood straps where required.
  • PermitPermit rules vary, and we confirm what the city requires.

Compare written quotes line by line, because a lower price can leave out parts you will need.

Energy savings and care

A new heater only saves money if it is set and maintained properly. A temperature near 120 degrees Fahrenheit is a common household target that balances scalding risk and comfort. Insulating the first few feet of hot pipe reduces waiting time. A yearly flush clears sediment, and an anode rod check extends tank life.

If you have recurring hot water complaints, a longer run from the heater to the far bathroom might be the cause, and a recirculation pump could help. We can quote that in the same visit.

Permits and codes in Duncanville

The City of Duncanville adopted the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC). For permit questions, call Permit and Inspection Services at (972) 780-5000, open Monday through Friday, 8 to 5, at City Hall, 203 E. Wheatland Road. Contractors register through the city's Citizen Access Portal.

Verify any plumber you hire by asking for the Responsible Master Plumber license number and checking it on tsbpe.texas.gov.

Questions homeowners ask

A like for like swap is often done in a half day. Moving the unit, adding a vent or changing fuel takes longer.

Pick what your house is already set up for unless a remodel gives you a reason to change. Gas recovers faster, electric is simpler to site.

If your plumbing is a closed system, with a check valve or pressure regulator at the main, an expansion tank is usually needed. We check this during the visit.
