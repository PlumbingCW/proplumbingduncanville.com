About Candlelight Estates

Candlelight Estates is a residential neighborhood in Duncanville, Texas, in ZIP code 75137. Real estate listings place homes on streets such as Charlotte Street and Candlelight Avenue. We have not found a published official boundary for the neighborhood, so we describe it by those anchor streets and do not claim exact edges.

One neighborhood directory, Neighborhoods.com, describes the area as a well-established community of single-family houses, mostly three bedrooms and two baths of roughly 1,244 to 1,411 square feet, built between 1971 and 1973. That is a third party summary that has not been verified by an association or the city, so treat it as a general picture and not as a record of any one house.

What a home from the early 1970s may need

A house that is more than fifty years old has usually had parts of its plumbing replaced at least once. Fixtures, shutoff valves, supply lines and the water heater are the usual items. We do not assume what is inside any particular house, because owners and previous owners have made different repairs over the decades.

What helps is a plain look at the parts you can see. Check under sinks and behind toilets for damp or stained cabinet floors, look at the shutoff valves for crusting or drips, and watch the water heater for rust on the tank or moisture at the base. These checks apply to any home and are not a claim about this neighborhood.

  • Running or leaking toilets and worn flush parts, see toilet repair.
  • Dripping faucets and shutoff valves that no longer close fully, see faucet repair.
  • Slow or repeated drain clogs in a kitchen or bathroom, see drain cleaning.
  • A water heater that leaks, makes noise or no longer heats, see water heater repair.

Questions to ask when you call

Having a few facts ready makes the call faster. Know roughly how old the water heater is, whether the problem affects one fixture or the whole house, and where your main water shutoff is. If water is spreading, close the main valve first and then call.
