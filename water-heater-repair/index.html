Emergency Water Heater Repair in Duncanville, TX

No hot water or a tank that is dripping? We repair gas, electric and hybrid water heaters in Duncanville, and we tell you honestly when repair is not worth it.

Call (972) 903-8224

A water heater that stops working is rarely convenient. Our 24/7 line takes calls for cold showers, pilot lights that will not stay lit and tanks that are dripping.

If water is pooling under the tank, shut off the cold supply valve above it, switch the gas to pilot or the breaker to off, and call. A leaking tank is an emergency, and a cold shower is a same day repair.

Water heater repair covers the parts that wear out before the tank does: thermostats, heating elements, igniters, thermocouples, gas valves, anode rods, relief valves and drain valves. When the tank itself has failed, repair is not the answer, and we say so plainly.

What to do when a water heater fails

Three situations need a plumber promptly. Water leaking from the tank body, which is almost always a failed tank. A relief valve that is steadily discharging, which signals excess pressure or temperature. And any smell of gas near a gas heater. For gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 before calling us.

A heater with no hot water, a tripped breaker or a pilot that goes out is urgent but not dangerous. Those calls are usually fixed in one visit when the part is on the truck.

Repair or replace, honestly

A repair makes sense when the tank is sound and the failed part is cheap. A replacement makes sense when the tank leaks, when it is well past its typical life, or when the repair would cost a large share of a new unit. We do not guess from the phone. A plumber checks the age on the data plate, tests the parts and gives you both numbers.

Tank age is easy to read. The serial number on the rating label encodes the build date, and the manufacturer's website explains the code. If the unit is old and leaking, spending on a part is wasted money.

What decides the price of a water heater repair

Repairs vary because parts and access vary. You get a clear price before work starts.

  • The partA thermostat or igniter is a modest part. A gas control valve costs more.
  • Tank typeGas, electric and hybrid units use different parts and different labor.
  • LocationA garage tank is simple to reach. A closet or attic unit takes longer.
  • Hour of the callAfter hours calls carry an after hours rate that is quoted first.

Always ask for the price in writing. Texas does not regulate plumbing prices.

Hard water, sediment and Duncanville tanks

Duncanville receives treated surface water bought from the City of Dallas. According to the 2022 Dallas Water Utilities report, the chloramine disinfectant averaged 2.16 parts per million. The report we found does not give a hardness figure, so we do not claim one. If you want to know your water's hardness, test your tap or ask your utility.

What we can say is that any tank collects sediment over the years, and a yearly flush slows that down. A heater that is never flushed tends to rumble and lose capacity sooner.

Water heater safety and local resources

Every tank should have a temperature and pressure relief valve with a discharge pipe that runs downward. If yours has no pipe or the pipe ends in a plug, call us. A plug can turn a relief valve into a hazard.

For questions about utility service or water quality in Duncanville, call Public Works at (972) 780-4900. Permit rules vary, so ask the Permit and Inspection Services office at (972) 780-5000 if your repair involves moving the heater.

If the tank has failed, see replacement. For a new unit chosen in advance, we have a page on water heater installation. We also serve Lancaster and Red Oak.
