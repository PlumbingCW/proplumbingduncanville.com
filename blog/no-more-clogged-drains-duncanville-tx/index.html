No More Clogged Drains in Duncanville, TX

Daily habits and yearly checks that keep drains running.

Call (972) 903-8224

Most clogged drains are not bad luck. They come from the same few causes, and most of those causes are avoidable. A home where the kitchen drain clogs every few months is telling you about grease. A shower that clogs weekly is telling you about hair. A main line that backs up once a year is telling you about roots or a damaged pipe.

This guide is a practical plan for Duncanville homeowners who want to stop calling a plumber for the same clog. It covers daily habits, a short yearly routine, what a plumber can do to break the cycle and which signs mean a bigger problem.

Daily habits that work

  • Scrape plates into the trash, not the sink
  • Pour cooled grease into a can and throw it away
  • Use a drain strainer in the kitchen sink and a hair catcher in the shower
  • Flush only toilet paper and waste, even if wipes are labeled flushable
  • Run hot water for a short time after washing dishes
  • Keep food scraps out of the disposal if they are fibrous or starchy

These habits cost little. A strainer is inexpensive, and a can for grease is free. Most clogs are the sum of many small deposits, not one large mistake.

A yearly routine

  1. 01Clean the stoppersRemove and clean sink and tub stoppers. A hooked tool pulls out hair at the top of the drain.
  2. 02Flush the kitchen lineRun very hot tap water for a minute after you have run the sink. Avoid chemical drain openers.
  3. 03Check for slow spotsTest each fixture for slow draining. A single slow drain is the early warning.
  4. 04Schedule a lookIf a line has a history, schedule a cleaning or a camera inspection before it fails.

Why drain openers are a poor plan

Liquid openers work by heat or chemistry, and they can damage older pipe and finishes. They also sit in a pipe that is still blocked, so the next person to work on it faces caustic liquid. A plunger, a hand auger and hot water do most of what a home fix can do without risk.

Enzyme cleaners are gentler and can help with maintenance of grease, but they are slow and will not clear an established clog.

When a plumber is the answer

A single clog that clears with a plunger is routine. A drain that clogs more than twice in a year needs a closer look. A main line that backs up needs a cable or a jet and a camera to find why. Drain cleaning clears the blockage. Hydro jetting cleans the pipe wall. A camera inspection shows if the pipe is damaged.

If the pipe is cracked or bellied, cleaning will not hold, and a repair is the lasting answer.

A simple log helps you spot the pattern

If a drain has clogged more than once, keep a short note on your phone. Write down the date, the fixture and what you think caused it. After three entries, a pattern often shows. A kitchen sink that clogs after big weekend meals points to grease.

A bathroom sink that clogs every other month points to the stopper. A toilet that clogs only when guests visit points to a weak flush or a partial blockage that shows up under extra load.

The log also helps a plumber. Telling dispatch that the same line has backed up three times since spring changes the tool we bring and the advice we give. It is the difference between clearing a clog and fixing a cause.

What a plumber can do that you cannot

A professional can see inside the line with a camera, clean it with a jetter and measure whether a section holds water. Those three steps turn a guess into a plan. If the pipe is sound, a yearly cleaning keeps it clear. If the pipe is damaged, a repair is the only lasting answer, and the camera tells you where.

A last word on habits. The simplest rule is that only water, toilet paper and human waste go down a toilet, and only water and a little soap go down a sink. Everything else belongs in the trash.

A household that follows this rule will see far fewer clogs than one that treats the drain as a disposal, and the cost of that discipline is a small trash can by the sink and one by the toilet.

Who maintains which pipe in Duncanville

If your house has no cleanout, ask Permit and Inspection Services about adding one under the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC) that the city adopted. The pipe from your house to the point where it joins the public line is usually the owner's to maintain.

If several homes on your street have the same backup, call Duncanville Public Works at (972) 780-4900 to ask who maintains the public line in your block.

For water emergencies after hours, the city line is (972) 780-4959. For a problem inside the house, call us on our 24/7 line.

Older homes need more attention

Duncanville grew from 3,774 people in 1960 to 35,008 in 1990, so many homes have drains that are decades old. Cast iron scales and clay admits roots. A yearly check on an older line costs far less than a weekend backup.

If your house is newer, the habit list still holds. Plastic pipes do not scale, but grease and wipes still clog them. Customers in DeSoto and Lancaster follow the same plan, and many of them have the same older cast iron lines.
