Where Swan Ridge is

Swan Ridge is a residential neighborhood in Duncanville, Texas, ZIP 75137. Property listings in the area use Mockingbird Court, Oak Leaf Drive and Meadowlark Lane. We found no official boundary, so these streets are anchors and not edges.

Homes.com lists brick houses on corner and cul-de-sac lots and townhomes on Oak Leaf Drive, with sample homes of about 1,900 to 2,400 square feet. It reports that about 85 percent of residents own their home and about 15 percent rent. That is third party data and not a count of any one street. The Swan Ridge name also appears as a Nextdoor neighborhood.

A mix of houses and townhomes

Houses and townhomes need slightly different plumbing conversations. In a house, you own and maintain the lines from the meter to the fixtures. In a townhome, a shared wall or a shared service may be involved, and your association or owner may decide who can authorize work.

  • A house with a leak under a sink, behind a toilet or at a water heater. See leak detection.
  • A kitchen sink that drains slowly or a garbage disposal that hums or leaks. See garbage disposal repair.
  • A bathroom drain that keeps slowing down. See drain cleaning.
  • A water heater that no longer heats. See water heater repair.

What to check before you call from a townhome

  1. 01Find your unit shutoffIt is often near the water heater or where the line enters the unit.
  2. 02Check for shared wallsA leak at a shared wall may affect a neighbor, so tell them as well.
  3. 03Contact your associationAsk who is responsible for the line and who authorizes a repair.
  4. 04Call and describe itSay where the water is and whether it is stopped.

Pool and yard plumbing

A sample listing in the area includes an inground pool. A pool brings its own equipment, and that is separate from household plumbing. If a yard line or spigot leaks, though, it is a normal supply line repair, and the water meter test of turning off every tap and watching the dial is a good first step.

Local notes
