Tankless Water Heater in Duncanville, TX

Endless hot water without a storage tank is possible, but only if the house has the gas or electrical capacity to support it.

Call (972) 903-8224

A tankless water heater heats water only when a tap is open. There is no storage tank to rust, leak or run out. A flow sensor starts a burner or heating elements, the water passes through a heat exchanger, and the heater shuts down when the flow stops. The promise is continuous hot water, a smaller footprint and lower standby loss.

The trade-offs are real. A tankless unit costs more to buy and install, it needs a bigger gas line or a much bigger electrical service than a tank, and it has a maximum flow. We explain those points before anything is ordered.

Is tankless right for your house

  • You use hot water for long showers, a soaking tub or several fixtures at once, and you want it to keep going
  • You are short on space and want the tank out of a closet or a garage
  • You are planning a remodel and can run a larger gas line or breaker
  • You can accept a higher upfront cost for lower running loss over time

Tankless is a poor fit when the electrical panel is full, the gas meter is too small, or the household has few hot water demands. In those cases a standard tank or a hybrid is more sensible, and we say so.

Gas versus electric tankless

FeatureGas tanklessElectric tankless
Hot water outputHigher flow for the whole houseLower flow for a whole house, good for a small home or a point of use
What the house needsA larger gas line, a vent and a condensate drain on high efficiency unitsA large dedicated 240 volt circuit and a panel with spare capacity
Typical installationVenting through a wall or roofNo vent, simpler siting
MaintenanceAnnual flush, vent and filter checkAnnual flush, check for scale on elements

Electric units are sized in kilowatts, and the current they draw follows from watts divided by volts. An 11.8 kilowatt unit on a 240 volt circuit draws about 49 amps, which is why the panel and wire gauge must be checked first. Ask us to read the label of the exact model before you buy.

Repairs include error codes, no hot water, fluctuating temperature, igniter failure, flow sensor faults and scale. Most tankless units show a code on a display. Write it down and tell dispatch when you call.

What decides the price of a tankless job

Tankless pricing is driven more by the house than the unit. You get a clear price before work starts.

  • Unit and capacityOutput, brand and features set the cost of the heater.
  • Gas or electrical upgradeA bigger gas line or a new breaker and wiring can be a large part of the job.
  • VentingStainless or PVC vent length and routing change the labor.
  • AccessoriesIsolation valves, a condensate neutralizer and a recirculation loop where wanted.

Ask for the full scope in writing so you can compare it with a tank quote.

Descaling and water quality

Mineral scale builds on a tankless heat exchanger and narrows the flow path. Flushing with a mild acid solution through the isolation valves removes it, and a yearly flush is a common recommendation. Duncanville's water comes from the City of Dallas and the 2022 report we reviewed lists no hardness figure, so test your tap to learn your own water and set the flush schedule from that.
