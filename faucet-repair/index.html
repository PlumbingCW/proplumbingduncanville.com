Emergency Faucet Repair in Duncanville, TX

From a spraying supply line to a drip that will not stop, we repair or replace faucets and the valves that serve them.

Call (972) 903-8224

A faucet that will not shut off or a supply line spraying under the sink is a call we answer 24/7. Turn the small oval valves under the sink clockwise to stop the water, and if they will not close, shut off the main valve at the house. Then call. A dripping faucet can wait for a daytime visit, but a spraying one cannot.

Faucet repair covers dripping spouts, worn cartridges, leaking handles, failed supply lines, corroded stop valves and low flow caused by clogged aerators. When a faucet is worn out, replacement can cost little more than a repair, and we explain both before you decide.

When a faucet problem is urgent

Water spraying from a line, a stop valve that will not close and a faucet that lets water pour under a cabinet all qualify. So does any leak that is soaking a cabinet base, because particleboard swells and rots quickly.

A slow drip is also worth fixing soon. A faucet that drips once per second wastes a few gallons per day, and the water bill reflects it. The fix is usually a cartridge or washer that costs little.

Corroded or stuck stop valves under a sink are a common problem in older homes. A valve that has not been turned in years can fail when you try to close it, and replacing it while the faucet is being fixed gives you a working shutoff for the next emergency.

How we repair a faucet

  1. 01Stop the waterWe close the stop valves, or the main when they fail, and protect the cabinet.
  2. 02DiagnoseWe check the cartridge, seals, supply lines and valve condition to find the real cause.
  3. 03Repair or replaceWe replace the worn part, or recommend a new faucet when parts are no longer made.
  4. 04Test and cleanWe test for leaks under pressure, check the flow and clean up before leaving.

What decides the price of a faucet repair

Faucet work is usually quick. You get a clear price before work starts.

  • Repair or new fixtureA cartridge is a small part. A new faucet adds the fixture price.
  • Valve conditionA failed stop valve adds a part and some labor.
  • TypeKitchen pull down and wall mounted faucets take longer than a basic bath faucet.
  • Time of the callAfter hours rates are quoted first.

Ask for the price in writing and ask about the parts warranty.

Outdoor faucets and hose bibs

Outdoor spigots fail in a different way. Hoses left attached through a cold snap can freeze and crack the pipe behind the wall, and the leak only shows when the water is turned back on. If an outside faucet drips inside the wall, shut off the supply and call us.

Frost proof bibs and vacuum breakers are the usual upgrades. A new bib is a small job, and a repair after a freeze can involve opening the wall.

Hard freezes and faucets in Duncanville

Winter Storm Uri ran from February 13 to 17, 2021, and cracked outdoor lines across Texas. Insulated covers and a disconnected hose are the cheapest protection when a freeze is forecast. Let a tap drip slowly and open cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls.
