What to tell dispatch

  • What is happening and where the water is going
  • Your street address and nearest cross street
  • Whether you have shut off the main valve
  • Whether anyone is in danger, or you smell gas, in which case leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 first

Before you call

According to the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners, you can verify any plumber at tsbpe.texas.gov. Close the main water valve if water is spreading, turn off the water heater if it is leaking and stay away from wet outlets. If you are not sure which service you need, our services page lists them all, and the service area page shows the towns we reach.
